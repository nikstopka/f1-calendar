Рассел поражен «безумной» тяговой мощностью в Лас-Вегасе

Mercedes' George Russell missed out on getting a tow during Qualifying at the Las Vegas Grand Prix, but the Briton will still line up on the front row for Saturday's race.

George Russell has admitted that he was surprised by the “insane” tow power in Qualifying at the Las Vegas Grand Prix, with the Mercedes racer set to start from the front row for Saturday’s race despite not benefitting from a slipstream as others did.

After topping the timesheets earlier on in the weekend’s third and final practice hour, Russell again looked to have good pace in Qualifying.

However, the Briton emerged on track in front of team mate Kimi Antonelli – who was set to serve a five-place grid penalty – during the first runs of Q3, meaning that he could not get a tow from the other Silver Arrows machine.

While he asked over the radio if he could go behind Antonelli, given the World Champion’s impending penalty, the squad ultimately did not use a tow – with Antonelli dropping back during the final runs and only just making the flag to start his lap, one that ultimately put him in P1 before his grid drop demoted him to P6.

Russell, meanwhile, went third fastest behind Red Bull’s Max Verstappen, meaning that he will start from P2 behind the Dutchman in Saturday’s race.

“It was a very strong session on the whole, but of course it came down to that slipstream and I don't think any of us anticipated how powerful it would be,” Russell reflected after Qualifying.

“It was insane. I still can't wrap my head around it. Kimi said he gained over one second to himself from the lap before, just in the straights.

"But it's high risk – if he started his lap just a few seconds later, he would have had no lap at all, so in the end I'm pleased with the session as a whole.”

Looking ahead to race day, the 28-year-old believes that a number of drivers could be in the fight for victory.

“I think it's going to be very close out there tomorrow,” Russell explained. “As I said, with the slipstream being so powerful, I think it's going to very challenging for the lead car, whoever it may be, to get away, so I think any drivers in the top five or six will be in contention.”
