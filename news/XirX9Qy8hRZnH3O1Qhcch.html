Во сколько начнется Гран-при Катара 2026 года и как его можно посмотреть?

Here are all the timings – along with all the additional information you need – for the 22nd Grand Prix weekend of the 2026 season from Qatar.

The 2026 Formula 1 season heads to Qatar this weekend for the penultimate race of the campaign, with the drivers and teams preparing to tackle the Lusail International Circuit.

Max Verstappen arrives in Qatar following his victory at the Las Vegas Grand Prix, where the Red Bull driver came out on top after an action-packed race that featured plenty of overtaking and battles throughout the field.

While Kimi Antonelli and Mercedes have already secured the Drivers' and Constructors' Championships respectively, the battle for third in the teams' standings remains finely poised, with just nine points separating Red Bull and McLaren heading into the final two rounds.

Head to the RACE HUB or scroll down for more on how and when you can watch the action, along with the global start times for each session.

What time is the Qatar Grand Prix?

The weekend will kick off with Free Practice 1 at 1630 local time on Friday, 27 November, followed by Free Practice 2 later that day at 2000.

A final hour of practice will begin at 1730 on Saturday, 28 November, before Qualifying gets underway at 2100. The Grand Prix itself will take place at 1900 on Sunday, 29 November.

How can I watch the Qatar Grand Prix?

You can enjoy coverage from every session live on F1 TV Pro (available in selected countries), which brings fans closer to the action via exclusive features like onboard cameras on all 22 of the drivers' cars, and access to both Pre-Race and Post-Race Shows.

F1 TV Pro can be streamed easily via Apple TV, Chromecast Generation 2 and above, Android TV, Google TV, Amazon Fire TV, and Roku. F1 TV Pro is free of ad breaks and available with commentary in six languages.

Fans can also watch via F1 TV Premium, a premier service that offers the ability to watch races in 4K Ultra HD/HDR on up to six different devices, as well as a personalised Multiview feature available on select devices with 26 different feeds to choose from.

Viewers can watch every F1 Grand Prix, F1 Sprint, Qualifying and practice session live in 4K Ultra HD/HDR at home, with F2, F3, F1 ACADEMY and Porsche Supercup sessions also available to watch live on the platform.

Alternatively, live timing will be available on F1.com and the F1 app across every session during the Grand Prix weekend.

Where is the 2026 Qatar Grand Prix taking place?

The Qatar Grand Prix will take place at the Lusail International Circuit, located on the outskirts of Doha, which first hosted Formula 1 in 2021.

Originally built for motorcycle racing, the 5.419-kilometre circuit features a series of fast, flowing corners, with medium- and high-speed turns making up much of the lap.

Its main straight stretches for more than one kilometre, providing one of the best overtaking opportunities into Turn 1, while the circuit's demanding layout poses a challenge for drivers and tyres alike.

The drivers will complete 57 laps of Lusail during Sunday's Grand Prix, covering a total race distance of 308.611 kilometres.

What is the weather forecast in Qatar?

Dry conditions are expected throughout the Qatar Grand Prix weekend, with little chance of rain across the three days of running at Lusail.

Temperatures are forecast to reach the mid-to-high 20s Celsius during the weekend, although conditions are expected to be slightly cooler for Saturday's Qualifying session and Sunday's race, both of which will take place under the floodlights.

When can I watch Formula 2 in Qatar?

Formula 2 will also be in action at the Lusail International Circuit, with the championship entering its penultimate round of the 2026 season.

The weekend gets underway with Free Practice at 1400 local time on Friday, 27 November, followed by Qualifying at 1800.

The 23-lap Sprint Race will take place at 1915 on Saturday, 28 November, before the 32-lap Feature Race gets underway at 1500 on Sunday, 29 November.

Fans can follow all the Formula 2 action live on F1 TV, where available.
