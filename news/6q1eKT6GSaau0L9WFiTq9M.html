«Большой риск» Антонелли оправдывается в квалификации в Лас-Вегасе

Kimi Antonelli topped Qualifying for the Las Vegas Grand Prix, but will start sixth after serving a grid penalty.

Kimi Antonelli admits that he "took a big risk" after finishing fastest in Qualifying for the Las Vegas Grand Prix, with the Mercedes driver also suggesting "I wasn't driving well".

The World Champion finished top of the times during Qualifying on Friday, beating Red Bull's Max Verstappen by just over two tenths.

But the Italian will start sixth for Saturday's Grand Prix after serving a five-place grid penalty for a turbo change ahead of the weekend.

Antonelli's fastest time came at the very end of the session, having only just made it onto his final flying lap as he and other drivers battled for position, with the tow proving especially effective along the Strip.

It meant Antonelli began his lap close to the car ahead, with the 20-year-old also struggling for grip throughout, making his efforts to finish P1 even more of a surprise.

When asked how satisfied he was with Qualifying, Antonelli responded: "Not really to be fair because I had no feeling with the car today in single lap."

"I couldn't get the tyres in the right window and was just really... I had zero confidence. I wasn't driving well, [I made] lots of mistakes.

"And that last lap I took a big risk, decided to back off and it's never ideal when you start the lap so close to the car in front because with the dirty air you slide a lot around. But overall it worked out and I'm happy with it."

The last time Antonelli served a grid penalty for a power unit change was in Italy, with the Mercedes driver storming through the field from P19 on the grid to take victory at home.

With Las Vegas' street circuit also offering the chance for overtakes, and with Antonelli only starting from P6, he remains hopeful of a strong result come the end of the Grand Prix.

"Definitely," said Antonelli when asked about fighting for a top result.

"I've been feeling more comfortable in long run, especially in FP3 so hopefully tomorrow we can get a good start and fight back and hopefully we can get a great result."
