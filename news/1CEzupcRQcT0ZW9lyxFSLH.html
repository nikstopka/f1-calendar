ГАЛЕРЕЯ: Знаменитости соревнуются друг с другом в гонке на LEGO в Лас-Вегасе

There was action even before the start of the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix, as 11 celebrities took part in the inaugural LEGO race.

The event took place as F1 and the LEGO Group announced the next phase of their successful licensing partnership, including the unveiling of the new Speed Champions collection, which features a minifigure of every F1 driver for the first time.

Following the hugely successful drivers’ parades in Miami and Silverstone, all 11 teams were represented during the celebrity race, which was won by Australian music producer Fisher for Red Bull at the wheel of a LEGO car made from more than 28,000 bricks.

Other well-known competitors included actor Terry Crew (Cadillac), chef Gordon Ramsay (McLaren), comedian Jack Whitehall (McLaren) and American gymnast Jordan Chiles (Haas) amongst others.
