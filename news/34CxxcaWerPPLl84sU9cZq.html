Леклер ищет ответы после непростой пятницы в Катаре

Charles Leclerc reflected on a challenging Friday for Ferrari at the Qatar Grand Prix, while Sporting Director Diego Ioverno offered an update on Lewis Hamilton’s reliability issue.

Charles Leclerc was left searching for answers after a difficult opening day at the Qatar Grand Prix, with the Ferrari driver struggling to understand the extent of Mercedes’ advantage.

Leclerc ended both of Friday’s sessions in seventh position, with team mate Lewis Hamilton eighth on both occasions, while an early spin for the former in FP2 resulted in floor damage that required repairs.

Asked to assess his Friday, the Monegasque said: “Honestly, it’s been a very, very difficult day. It’s not that we didn’t try on the soft, we couldn’t improve on our lap time on the medium.

“I lost the car and had some damage, so we had to repair the car, but that was on the first lap of FP2. Then I couldn’t improve, and even on the new soft tyre later on."

He continued: “The feeling is very difficult to get right and not even on the high fuel where we are strong. For now it looks like a very difficult weekend, but we are working hard to try and understand.

“For some reason, Mercedes are so much faster than everybody expected, so the gaps are a little bit strange. There’s something that is the outlier, we just need to find that.”

When asked to expand on his off-track moment in FP2, Leclerc explained: “I tried to go a little bit early on the throttle. It’s a track where there are some corners where you can take a bit more risk because obviously you’ve got a lot of space, and this is one of those, so I tried to go early on the throttle and I lost the car.”

It was an equally challenging day on the other side of the Ferrari garage, with Hamilton having reported issues with the balance of his car during second practice before a loss of power in the closing stages forced the seven-time World Champion to stop on track, bringing out the Virtual Safety Car.

However, Ferrari Sporting Director Diego Ioverno played down concerns over Hamilton’s reliability issue heading into Saturday, although the Italian highlighted the lack of data available to the team following their disrupted running.

“Lewis had a reliability problem that we are investigating,” Ioverno explained. “That is not a concern for tomorrow, but for sure was a concern for the session.

“All in all, not the best day for us. Not so much data to analyse, but anyhow, a long night for us trying to do better tomorrow.”

With just one practice session remaining before Qualifying, Ioverno also outlined the challenges facing Ferrari as they look to close the gap to their rivals.

“The competition seems quite difficult for us so far,” he added. “We’ll try to gather some more data tomorrow in FP3.

“That most likely will be for quali preparation because here is quite a quali-biased event. The others seem strong. We’ll try to do what we can to close the gap... It’s going to be a challenging weekend.”
