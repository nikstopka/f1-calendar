Норрис обеспокоен отставанием McLaren от Mercedes в Катаре

Lando Norris believes McLaren are fighting for P3 at the Qatar Grand Prix after Mercedes led the way in both of Friday’s practice sessions.

Lando Norris has admitted that McLaren could be fighting for P3 at best in Qatar with the Briton concerned by the team’s lack of pace relative to Mercedes despite an encouraging showing in Friday’s practice sessions.

Norris was third fastest during the second practice session at the Lusail International Circuit, finishing 0.445s adrift of Kimi Antonelli, with the Italian having set his fastest time on the medium tyres while the McLaren driver was on the softs.

With Mercedes having recorded a 1-2 in both of Friday’s sessions, Norris believes that McLaren are more closely matched with Red Bull and Ferrari heading into the remainder of the weekend.

“I don’t think we’re too bad relative to most, but to Mercedes, I don’t think we’ve been further off,” Norris admitted. “They just seem in a different category compared to us today, quicker in every single corner by a mile.

“So I would say the battle is for P3, and we seem in a relatively decent position. We seem similar in pace to Red Bull and similar-ish to Ferrari.

“There’s things we need to improve and things that can certainly be better. But I guess you always look to the top, so when you look at ourselves compared to Mercedes, we need to improve a lot everywhere.”

Having run an older specification of the MCL40’s floor on Friday, McLaren are set to switch to the newer version ahead of Saturday’s running, although Norris is doubtful that it will allow the team to take the challenge to the Silver Arrows.

“I don’t know how much [the new floor] will help around here, relative to Vegas, but I’ll see tonight and see how much we can gain from that,” he explained.

“It’s not going to be the gap that we need. I would say we’re around six, seven tenths, probably seven tenths off Mercedes a lap, so it’s not even worth thinking about them. I think relative to the rest, we seem like we’re in a good place. The fact that we can have the new floor tomorrow will add a little bit, but a little bit is always better than nothing.”

McLaren’s Racing Director, Randy Singh, explained that the decision to run the older floor specification on Friday was taken to protect the new floor from potential damage. The high speed corner characteristics of the Lusail International circuit can be brutal on the cars, and risk damaging floor components as drivers go over the kerbs on exit.

“Lots of high-speed corners here and some lower-speed corners, so getting the balance right is very tricky across the whole lap,” Singh explained.

“We ran the older-spec floor today, mostly just because you can see people running wide, damaging floors, and we didn’t want to damage any of the newer-spec floors. The new spec will be on tomorrow. It won’t be a massive difference, but it’s all small things that push us in the right direction.”

With Norris’ team mate Oscar Piastri finishing just 0.027s behind the Briton in fourth and Red Bull’s Max Verstappen a further 0.027s adrift in fifth, Singh is anticipating a closely fought Qualifying session.

“I think the top four teams will be close,” he added. “Maybe Mercedes will be a small step ahead. I think it’ll be very tight with us and Red Bull, and probably with Ferrari tomorrow.

“So it’s very hard to say putting the new floor on will do this or that, or even the changes we make overnight. I think it’s going to be tight, like it has been for this part of the season.”
