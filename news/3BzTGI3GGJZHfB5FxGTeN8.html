5 гонщиков, у которых больше всего шансов на победу в Катаре

We look at the betting odds and analyse the chances of the five drivers most likely to win at the Lusail International Circuit.

Max Verstappen won an action-packed Las Vegas Grand Prix last weekend and the Dutch star will be looking to add to his string of successes in Qatar.

The Red Bull driver has won the last three editions of the Qatar Grand Prix at the Lusail International Circuit and arrives as the man in form after taking the chequered flag in Nevada.

Verstappen leads the betting market to win Sunday’s race, while new F1 World Champion Kimi Antonelli is second favourite with bookmakers as he bids to end a run of three races without a win.

Our expert betting writers have looked at the F1 markets and assessed the chances of the five drivers who are most likely to win in Qatar.

Max Verstappen (Red Bull)

This list has a different look from the past few races, with Ferrari's disappointing performance in Las Vegas leading to Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton being ranked only seventh and eighth favourite for success in Qatar, but there is no surprise about the name at the top.

Verstappen won for the fourth time in six races in Las Vegas after inheriting pole position due to Antonelli's grid penalty and he could be hard to stop.

The Red Bull star has won the last three races at the Lusail International Circuit and is now on a run of nine consecutive podium finishes.

He is the 7/5 (2.40, +140) favourite as he looks to wrap up second place in the Drivers' Championship standings before the finale in Abu Dhabi.

Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes)

The newly-crowned World Champion had an interesting evening in Las Vegas, making impressive ground after a poor start before a late power deployment issue left him off the podium in fourth.

After producing the fastest lap in Qualifying, he is hard to rule out this week as there is no grid penalty hanging over his Saturday performance this week.

Antonelli finished fifth in Qatar in his rookie season but has now gone three races without a top-two finish for only the second time in his unbeatable second campaign.

He is a bigger price than usual this week but is the second favourite to win in Qatar at odds of 11/4 (3.75, +375).

Lando Norris (McLaren)

Lando Norris has now secured consecutive top-two finishes for only the second time this season, winning in Sao Paulo before finishing second to Verstappen in Las Vegas.

Norris has finished third in Qatar three times, although two of them came in Sprints, while last season he qualified second at Lusail but finished only fourth after his team took a costly decision not to pit under a Safety Car.

McLaren have shown speed at this track and Norris has looked strong in the last two rounds, so a fourth Norris win this season has to be considered at respectful odds of 11/2 (6.50, +550).

George Russell (Mercedes)

George Russell had another tough weekend in Las Vegas, starting on the front row after qualifying third-fastest and leading the race for brief periods before a late lock-up sent him into the barrier and he finished ninth.

The British driver doesn't have a great record in Qatar, having never finished higher than fourth, and he was only sixth last season.

His Mercedes speed makes Russell a contender and he is fourth in the race betting at 17/2 (9.50, +850).

Oscar Piastri (McLaren)

Oscar Piastri hasn't regularly appeared in the top five of the early markets this season but the Australian's impressive podium in Las Vegas has led to bookmakers taking him more seriously this week.

Piastri overcame a 10-place grid penalty that led him to start in 17th to make his way through the field to third place in Nevada, where he landed his first podium since Miami in May.

The Australian is something of a Lusail specialist, having won three Sprints at the track, while he was on pole last year. With a 100 per cent record of finishing on the podium in Qatar, Piastri is fifth in the betting markets this week at 10/1 (11.0, +1000).
