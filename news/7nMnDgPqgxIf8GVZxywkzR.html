Хэмилтон определяет «главную цель» перед Гран-при Лас-Вегаса

Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc are targeting a strong end to 2026 as Ferrari fight for P2 and look ahead to next season.

Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc have stressed the importance of finishing the 2026 season strongly as Ferrari look to bounce back from a challenging Sao Paulo Grand Prix, with the team focused on securing P2 in the Constructors' Championship and gathering valuable information ahead of next year.

Ferrari arrive in Las Vegas after Leclerc and Hamilton finished eighth and ninth respectively in Brazil, but Hamilton remains determined to deliver a result that would mean a great deal to those working behind the scenes at Maranello.

"The Constructors' Championship is the main focus," Hamilton said during media day in Las Vegas. "The main focus is making sure we solidify second in the Constructors' for the team, because that's something that means a huge amount to everyone back at the factory, but especially to me also.

"That's what we're working towards and then, if we're able to do well and keep in the battle for these last few races, maybe we can climb back up from fourth and hopefully be in the top three."

Looking ahead to 2027, Hamilton explained that the remaining three races would also offer opportunities to experiment with different setups and gather data, despite Ferrari having no new components available to test.

"This is a really important period of time," he explained. "We don't have any new components to test, but this is definitely a really important data point for us to understand and make some changes setup-wise, and try some things that we can potentially use to see if everything's working.

"There's some big changes for next year. But otherwise, right now we also have the focus on trying to make sure we maintain second in the Constructors' Championship. That's the focus."

Leclerc echoed his team mate's desire to finish the campaign positively, explaining that maintaining momentum heading into the winter break would be important for morale within Ferrari, while admitting that he is more concerned with securing further victories than his final Drivers' Championship position.

"My main focus at the moment is to try and win races, and the natural result of winning races will hopefully be second place in the Drivers' Championship," he said.

"I just want to finish the season on a high, for sure, in order to keep that good momentum. It's always important to finish the season on a high, particularly because the winter break is quite long, and for the motivation of everyone, it would be nice."

However, the Monegasque driver acknowledged that Ferrari could face another challenging weekend in Las Vegas, with cold temperatures and the circuit's long straights presenting particular concerns following the team's struggles with tyre warm-up in Brazil.

"We don't have any quick fixes, otherwise we would have put them on the car a long time ago," Leclerc admitted.

"There are things we cannot change now, and there are things that we can control and put our focus on. And that is the tyre warm-up, for example, that will be quite critical.

"If we do a very good job on that, I think it can outweigh maybe the disadvantage that we have power unit-wise. I remain hopeful, but it's going to be a big challenge for us."
