Путь Тото Вольффа от Williams до девяти титулов в Формуле-1

После девятой победы Mercedes в Кубке конструкторов мы вспоминаем путь Тото Вольффа от Williams до одного из самых успешных руководителей команд Формулы-1.

Приведя команду Mercedes к девятому Кубку конструкторов, Тото Вольфф сравнялся с сэром Фрэнком Уильямсом в истории Формулы-1. Вот как австриец стал одним из самых успешных руководителей команд Формулы-1.

Когда Mercedes завоевал свой девятый Кубок конструкторов на Гран-при Сан-Паулу 2026 года, это стало еще одной важной вехой в карьере руководителя команды Тото Вольффа.

В результате «Серебряные стрелы» сравнялись с командой Williams по количеству титулов в Кубке конструкторов, которых у них теперь девять, и уступили в этом показателе только командам Ferrari и McLaren за всю историю Формулы-1. Для Вольффа это достижение имело особое значение, учитывая его начало карьеры в этом виде спорта.

Прежде чем возглавить команду Mercedes, которая установила рекорд, восемь раз подряд выиграв Кубок конструкторов, этот австриец сделал свои первые шаги в менеджменте Формулы-1 в команде Williams, работая вместе с ее легендарным основателем сэром Фрэнком Уильямсом.

Теперь, спустя более десяти лет после прихода в Mercedes, Вольфф повторил достижение Фрэнка — девять титулов в Кубке конструкторов. Он вернул команду из Брэкли на вершину после четырех сезонов без чемпионских титулов.

Но как бывший гонщик и бизнесмен стал одним из самых успешных руководителей команд Формулы-1?

Из гонщика в бизнесмена

Вольфф родился в Вене в 1972 году, и его карьера в автоспорте началась за рулем, а не на стене пит-лейна. Проявив интерес к гонкам в подростковом возрасте, он принял участие в австрийской Формуле-Форд и одержал победу в своем классе на гонке «24 часа Нюрбургринга» 1994 года.

В рамках своей гоночной карьеры он также принял участие в чемпионате FIA GT в 2004 году, хотя к тому моменту Вольфф уже начал делать себе имя в деловом мире, понимая, что его таланты простираются далеко за пределы кокпита.

Он основал инвестиционные компании Marchfifteen в 1998 году и Marchsixteen в 2004 году, которые занимались технологическим и промышленным бизнесом, при этом сохраняя свои связи с автоспортом.

Эти доли включали HWA AG, компанию, ответственную за программы Mercedes-Benz в DTM и Формуле-3, что дало Вольффу опыт в коммерческой и соревновательной сферах автоспорта до того, как он стал руководителем команды Формулы-1.

Finding his feet at Williams

Wolff's involvement in Formula 1 began in 2009 when he invested in Williams, a team already established as one of the most successful in the sport's history.

Founded by Sir Frank Williams and Sir Patrick Head, the outfit had won nine Constructors' Championships between 1980 and 1997, enjoying success with drivers including Nigel Mansell, Alain Prost, Damon Hill and Jacques Villeneuve.

Although Williams were no longer regularly challenging for titles when Wolff arrived, the Austrian developed a close relationship with Frank, eventually becoming the team's Executive Director in 2012.

Reflecting on their relationship in a 2020 interview with F1.com, Wolff recalled the moment they first met.

"Frank Williams is an incredible person, with an aura and charisma that very few people in Formula 1 possess. I was lucky enough to meet him personally in 2009, and I'll never forget the first thing he said to me: 'I heard you can help me repay my mortgage!'"

Wolff's time at Williams would prove relatively brief, however, as another opportunity emerged in 2013.

A new chapter with Mercedes

In February 2013, Wolff joined Mercedes as Managing Partner, acquiring a 30% stake in the Formula 1 operation and taking on wider responsibilities for the manufacturer's motorsport activities. The move placed him alongside three-time World Champion Niki Lauda and Team Principal Ross Brawn as Mercedes continued building towards the front of the grid.

Wolff's first season coincided with Lewis Hamilton arriving at Mercedes to join Nico Rosberg, beginning one of the most successful partnerships in the sport's history.

Mercedes secured three victories during the 2013 campaign, with Rosberg winning in Monaco and Great Britain before Hamilton took victory in Hungary. The team eventually finished second in the Constructors' Championship behind Red Bull.

However, the introduction of new turbo-hybrid power unit regulations in 2014 would transform the competitive order.

Mercedes won 16 of the season's 19 Grands Prix, while Hamilton secured his second Drivers' Championship after a season-long battle with Rosberg. The result also delivered the team's first Constructors' title.

It was the beginning of an unprecedented period of success.

Eight consecutive championships

Mercedes' dominance continued throughout the following seasons, as Wolff oversaw a team that established a new benchmark for consistency in Formula 1.

Hamilton added further Drivers' Championships in 2015, 2017, 2018, 2019 and 2020, while Rosberg defeated his childhood friend and team mate to claim the 2016 crown. And while despite the intense rivalry between their drivers, Mercedes maintained their position at the front, navigating regulation changes and challenges from Ferrari and Red Bull.

By 2021, the Silver Arrows had won eight consecutive Constructors' Championships, a record unmatched by any other Formula 1 team.

Although Hamilton narrowly missed out on an eighth Drivers' title to Max Verstappen that season, Mercedes secured another teams' crown, extending their extraordinary run of success.

A ninth title and a place alongside Frank Williams

Mercedes' remarkable winning streak ended in 2022, as the introduction of new ground effect regulations changed the competitive landscape and left the team struggling to match their rivals.

For Wolff, it marked the beginning of a challenging period, with Mercedes managing just five Grand Prix victories across the following four seasons. Despite the setbacks, the Austrian remained at the helm, overseeing changes to the team's driver line-up and preparations for another major regulation overhaul in 2026.

Hamilton's departure for Ferrari at the end of 2024 also marked the conclusion of one of Formula 1's most successful driver-team partnerships, with Wolff choosing to promote young Italian Kimi Antonelli alongside George Russell.

"I made up my mind five minutes after Lewis Hamilton told me that he's going to Ferrari," said Wolff following Antonelli's announcement in August 2024.

"That is the line-up with these two [Russell and Antonelli] that I always wanted, bearing in mind the fast-tracking that we did with Kimi and everything related there too, but that was immediately what I wanted to do."

The decision represented a significant show of faith in the then 18-year-old, who had progressed through Mercedes' junior programme before making his Formula 1 debut in 2025.

“Wolff's decision was vindicated in 2026, with Antonelli securing his maiden Drivers' Championship at the Mexico City Grand Prix, becoming the youngest World Champion in F1 history at just 20 years old.”

Mercedes' resurgence was completed at the following round in Sao Paulo, where Russell and Antonelli's fourth and fifth-place finishes secured the team's ninth Constructors' Championship, ending a four-year wait for another title.

The achievement marked Mercedes' ninth Constructors' Championship, their first since 2021, and brought Wolff level with Sir Frank Williams, whose nine titles had been won across the 1980s and 1990s.

Speaking to Sky Sports following the race, Wolff reflected on the significance of matching the man who had helped him establish his career in Formula 1.

"Obviously, Frank is such an unbelievable icon of the sport, and to be tied on constructors' championships, I never thought that was ever possible.

"I had a close relationship with Frank, and to be there, it's amazing. I can't believe it."
