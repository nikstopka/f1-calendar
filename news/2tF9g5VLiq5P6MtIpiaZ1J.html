Аджар «не имеет понятия» о причине поломки переднего антикрыла в Лас-Вегасе

Isack Hadjar looked on course for a second podium on the bounce, but a broken front wing denied the Red Bull driver in Las Vegas.

Isack Hadjar conceded he was still "waiting for the team to tell me what happened" after his front wing broke during the Las Vegas Grand Prix, denying the Red Bull driver a chance of a podium.

The Frenchman featured in the lead battle during the early stages of the 50-lap race on the streets of Las Vegas, moving up from his starting position of P4 to challenge Red Bull team mate Max Verstappen for the lead.

After a mid-race pit stop under the Safety Car, Hadjar rejoined in third and immediately looked to challenge George Russell's Mercedes at the restart, only for the front wing on his car to collapse on the approach to Turn 5.

"I'm waiting for the team to tell me what happened at the moment. I just am clueless," said Hadjar.

"I actually didn't touch anything and the car just broke, so at the moment I'm just really curious. I know there was a massive bump on the track.

"I was braking, maybe that's what caused the issue but I don't really know now. But anyway, I had fun up to that point."

After pitting for a new front wing, Hadjar circulated towards the back of the field before eventually crossing the line in P14, only to drop a further position after receiving a 10-second penalty post-race.

This was after Hadjar had backed the pack up when the Safety Car was deployed in order to create a gap to Verstappen ahead as both drivers headed for the pits.

The stewards noted that "Car 6 significantly reduced its speed, at one point travelling approximately 100 km/h slower than Car 3, which was ahead of it".

The document added: "The Stewards considered that Car 6 was driving unnecessarily slowly for tactical reasons and that such conduct constituted a breach of the applicable regulations."
