Берман разочарован задержкой с подтверждением его будущего в Haas

Ollie Bearman is yet to have his future at Haas officially confirmed for 2027, a situation that the Briton says has been "a bit disappointing".

Ollie Bearman has admitted that he is “disappointed” by the delay in confirming his future at Haas, with the Briton conceding that the impact on himself and within the team is “not ideal”.

After making substitute appearances for the squad in 2024, Bearman made his full-time debut for Haas as the 2025 season kicked off and went on to earn a sophomore campaign with the American outfit.

However, while most of the grid has been made official for 2027, Haas are yet to confirm that Bearman will remain with the team despite the expectation being that the two parties will continue on together.

Quizzed during media day at the Las Vegas Grand Prix on whether it is disturbing from his perspective to not have the situation resolved yet, Bearman responded: “I would say yes.

“It's certainly a bit disappointing, and when you have people within the team questioning why you haven't been announced yet and things like that, it's certainly not ideal. Not only for them, but also for myself from a mental side. I wouldn't say it's the best to be standing here at round 20-something without any confirmation.

“We're working on it, and all I can say is that I'm a bit disappointed that I'm standing here without anything to tell you, because there's no excuses for that.”

Pushed on the idea that the delay could lead some to speculate that there is something wrong, Bearman said: “Which is not true, but things have dragged on a lot and I don't like that.”

The 21-year-old went on to suggest that he will be staying with Haas, but that the lack of official confirmation was tough to take.

“It's just a difficult situation, and I don't think there's any excuse to be standing here at this stage without anything to tell you,” he explained.

“That's shown by the fact that I'm the only guy who hasn't been announced for next year. Even if I can tell you that, touch wood, I'm going to be driving for Haas next year, the fact that it's not official is just disappointing.”

In terms of what is holding up the situation, Bearman commented: “I don't know. I don't know.”

Looking ahead to the shorter-term future and the Las Vegas Grand Prix, the Haas driver admitted that he is expecting a “tough weekend” but spoke of being glad to be back after a challenging triple header.

“To be honest, the previous few races have been really difficult,” said Bearman. “In Austin and Mexico, we kind of lost the chance of points, and on top of that, I was very sick in Mexico and Brazil as well. So it really sucked, and I was excited to get back home.

“It was a nice recharge for me these past few days, and I'm excited to be back with a lot of energy and hoping for a better final three events.”
