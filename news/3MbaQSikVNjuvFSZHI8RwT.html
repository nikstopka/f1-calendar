ВИКТОРИНА: Как много вы знаете о гонщиках в «клубе 300» Формулы-1?

This weekend at his home race Sergio Perez will compete in his 300th Grand Prix. But how much do you know about the other drivers in Formula 1's '300 club'?

Some drivers only ever get a handful of Grand Prix appearances under their belts, some might get a full season, others might get several years – but a select few manage to stick around in the top tier of motorsport for long enough to join the so-called '300 club' of drivers to have competed in 300 Grands Prix or more.

This weekend at his home race in Mexico City, Sergio Perez will become the latest inductee to join that exclusive club.

The Cadillac racer began his Formula 1 career way back in 2011 and has since racked up hundreds of Grand Prix appearances for the likes of Sauber, McLaren, Force India/Racing Point and Red Bull Racing.

There are a small number of other drivers who have already ticked past the 300 GP mark – some of them still competing in F1 today – so we thought a little quiz on those drivers would be a good way to celebrate Checo hitting the big 3-0-0.
