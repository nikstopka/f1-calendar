5 пилотов, которые с наибольшей вероятностью покажут лучшее время в квалификации в Катаре

We have looked at the betting markets for Qualifying at the Lusail International Circuit and assessed the chances of the leading contenders for pole position.

Formula 1 betting fans have been debating who should be credited as the polesitter for the Las Vegas Grand Prix after Kimi Antonelli set the fastest time in Qualifying but Max Verstappen started at the front of the grid.

There is only one correct answer with F1 bookmakers as the market is called ‘Fastest Qualifier’ and Antonelli was settled as the winner, with any penalties irrelevant even when, as in this case, they were known in advance.

After another close Qualifying battle between Mercedes, Red Bull and McLaren drivers in Las Vegas, fans will be eagerly awaiting the next test of speed in Qatar on Saturday.

With Lewis Hamilton, Lando Norris and Antonelli having gone fastest in Qualifying in the last three rounds, another fierce competition is expected and Verstappen is favourite to claim pole in his own right.

Our expert betting writers have looked at the Qualifying odds and assessed the chances of the leading contenders for pole in Qatar.

Max Verstappen (Red Bull)

Verstappen has regularly been favourite for pole in recent races but hasn't been the fastest qualifier since Singapore.

Bookmakers have still placed him at the top of the market for Qualifying again this week, and he is the hot favourite at 6/4 (2.50, +150).

The Red Bull star won from pole in Qatar in 2023, and he was also fastest in Qualifying in 2024, although he started that race from second after receiving a one-place penalty for driving unnecessarily slowly in Q3.

He brought up a hat-trick of Qatar wins in 2025, but had only been third-fastest behind both McLaren drivers in Qualifying on that occasion.

Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes)

Antonelli qualified fastest for just the second time in the past 11 races in Las Vegas, but that impressive showing has earned him the place as second-favourite for pole in Qatar at odds of 9/4 (3.25, +225).

The Italian star was only fifth and seventh in Sprint Qualifying and Grand Prix Qualifying at the track last season, but it's worth remembering how he totally left his rookie form behind him as he stormed to becoming F1’s youngest World Champion this season.

Lando Norris (McLaren)

Norris has never taken pole for the Qatar Grand Prix but has been third and second in Qualifying there in the last two seasons, and took pole for the Sprint in Lusail in 2024.

The McLaren driver therefore has to be taken seriously in Qualifying this week, particularly after a lively showing in the last two rounds as he took pole in Sao Paulo and was a competitive fourth on Friday night in Las Vegas.

McLaren took pole for the Sprint and the Grand Prix in Lusail last season through Oscar Piastri, and while the Australian is only eighth in the Qualifying betting at 18/1 (19.0, +1800), Norris is seen by bookmakers as a realistic contender for pole at 5/1 (6.0, +500).

George Russell (Mercedes)

George Russell hasn't taken a pole since his fifth Qualifying success of the season in Azerbaijan six races ago but with Mercedes having been the most successful team in Qualifying this season, he is fourth in the ‘Fastest Qualifier’ market this week.

Russell is 9/1 (10.0, +900) to go fastest on Saturday and he has shown himself well over a single lap at this track before, starting from the front row in 2023 and 2024.

Charles Leclerc (Ferrari)

Ferrari had another weekend to forget in Las Vegas, but Charles Leclerc makes the top five list for Qualifying success in Qatar with bookmakers.

The Monegasque driver is considered an outsider at 14/1 (15.0, +1400), which is the same price as Ferrari team mate Lewis Hamilton and Red Bull’s Isack Hadjar, and ahead of last year's Lusail polesitter Piastri.

Leclerc has still not added to his record of 27 pole positions this season and has never qualified higher than fifth in Qatar.
