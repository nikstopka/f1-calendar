5 пилотов, которые вернутся в квалификацию в Лас-Вегасе

We have looked at the betting markets for Qualifying at the Las Vegas Strip Circuit and assessed the chances of the leading contenders for pole.

After Lando Norris converted pole position into his third win of the season in the challenging conditions at the Sao Paulo Grand Prix last time out, the Formula 1 teams now head to Las Vegas as the final triple-header of the 2026 season gets underway.

This weekend, the drivers take on the famous Las Vegas Strip Circuit with its 6.201km track and the famous landmarks, followed by a quick stop in Qatar before the season finale at the Abu Dhabi Grand Prix in December.

2026 will be the fourth time that F1 cars have raced around this Vegas track, with Max Verstappen winning twice in 2023 and 2025 either side of George Russell’s 2024 success.

In two of those previous three races, the winning driver started from the front row, meaning that Qualifying in Vegas this weekend will be a hotly-contested session.

Russell made a clean getaway from the front of the grid in 2024 and held onto the lead to take the chequered flag by more than seven seconds, whilst Verstappen won from second last season and third in 2023.

Our expert betting writers have looked at the Qualifying odds and assessed the chances of the leading contenders for pole at the Las Vegas Strip Circuit.

Max Verstappen (Red Bull)

Verstappen is 13/8 (2.63, +163) to take pole position this weekend, despite the Red Bull driver never qualifying on pole in Vegas and only having two poles to his name this season.

However, Red Bull’s benchmark-setting power unit could be a major advantage on the Las Vegas Strip Circuit, with the 1.9km-long straight down The Strip being one of the longest flat-out sections on the calendar.

That, plus the two shorter straights elsewhere on the circuit, could give Verstappen the upper hand as he looks to consolidate second place in the Drivers’ Championship.

Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes)

Kimi Antonelli took pole at six of the first 10 races this season but has only been the fastest qualifier once in the 10 races since.

However, like Verstappen, the 5/2 (3.5, +250) shot can benefit from Mercedes’ extraordinary power-unit performance in Las Vegas.

The Silver Arrows have been largely dominant on power-focused circuits and, while their advantage has diminished during the second half of the season, they still have arguably the best-performing power unit on the grid.

George Russell (Mercedes)

Mercedes’ strength on the straights have also boosted Russell’s hopes of a sixth pole position of the season in Las Vegas, with the Briton priced at 6/1 (7.0, +600) to emerge the fastest in Qualifying.

Russell won the 2024 Las Vegas Grand Prix from pole and took pole in the Azerbaijan Grand Prix, which is probably the most comparable circuit to the Las Vegas Strip Circuit.

His performances have been underwhelming in recent races, but he has the ability to challenge at the front this weekend.

Lando Norris (McLaren)

Norris claimed his fourth pole and his third win of the season in Sao Paulo and is 6/1 (7.0, +600) to be the fastest qualifier this weekend.

The 2025 World Champion qualified on pole in last year’s Las Vegas Grand Prix and his four pole positions have come in the last 10 Grands Prix.

However, despite having the Mercedes power unit installed, McLaren’s shorter gear ratios have hampered their straight-line speed and that could hold Norris back in Vegas.

Charles Leclerc & Lewis Hamilton (Ferrari)

Charles Leclerc and Lewis Hamilton are both 10/1 (11.0, +1000) to claim Ferrari’s second pole position of the season but, like McLaren, they could be hampered on the long Las Vegas straights.

Ferrari sacrificed their turbocharger performance in favour of a quicker getaway off the line this season and that has limited their straight-line speed.

We have seen the Prancing Horse struggle at Monza and Azerbaijan, and this poor performance on the straights make them an outside bet to qualify fastest in Vegas.
