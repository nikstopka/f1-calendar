Камара предлагает обновленную информацию о планах на финальные гонки 2026 года

Rafael Camara reflects on his F1 debut in Brazil as he prepares for the Las Vegas Grand Prix, with his plans for Qatar and Abu Dhabi still undecided.

Rafael Camara is preparing for his second Formula 1 appearance with Haas at the Las Vegas Grand Prix, but a decision is yet to be reached on whether he will contest the final two rounds of the season.

Camara believes he will be better prepared for his second F1 outing after an intense debut in Sao Paulo, while admitting his plans for the final two rounds of the season remain undecided.

The 21-year-old Brazilian made his F1 debut at Interlagos following Esteban Ocon's departure from Haas, navigating challenging weather conditions before finishing 20th in his first Grand Prix.

Having been confirmed for another appearance alongside team mate Ollie Bearman in Las Vegas, Camara is hoping to build on the lessons learned in Brazil as he tackles another unfamiliar circuit.

"It was a very intense weekend, but there was a lot to learn, and it will be very useful this weekend," he said. "I'm excited to race here. Obviously, it will be my first time in Vegas, so I'm trying to prepare in the best way."

Unlike his last-minute call-up in Brazil, Camara has had more time to familiarise himself with the demands of a Grand Prix weekend, leaving him feeling more at ease ahead of his second outing.

"Mentally, it will be easier because I've had more time to prepare and everything feels a bit more normal," he explained. "Having already experienced some things in Brazil, I'm calmer and know a bit more about what to expect. I think it's just maybe thinking less and doing the driving more."

While Camara is confirmed to race for Haas in 2027, he admitted a decision is yet to be made on whether he will contest the final two Grands Prix in Qatar and Abu Dhabi or return to Formula 2.

"That is a good question, even to myself," he admitted. "It will be tricky to decide, but there's still a lot to happen. I'm just focused on finishing this weekend in a good way, and then we'll see what happens for the next two races."

The Brazilian added that discussions with his team would follow the Las Vegas weekend, with both options appealing to him.

"Both would be very important to me, but I still need to discuss with my team what we'll do for the next races," he said. "I would be happy with either."

Camara also spoke positively about his relationship with Haas Team Principal Ayao Komatsu, revealing that the pair had discussed his debut and the areas where he could improve.

"Overall, on this first weekend, he didn't complain about anything, so that was good," he said. "On Sunday, there were some things I would have expected to do better. But overall, it was a good weekend and very positive."
