Ферстаппен и Red Bull уехали из Катара в поисках баланса болида

Max Verstappen and Red Bull were unable to match the pace of Kimi Antonelli and Mercedes during Friday practice in Qatar.

Max Verstappen admitted that he and Red Bull had "a bit of a difficult day" during Friday practice ahead of the Qatar Grand Prix.

The four-time World Champion was only able to finish P6 and P5 quickest across the two, one-hour sessions at the Lusail International Circuit, having struggled with understeer throughout the day.

"A bit of a difficult day. The balance of the car is just probably a bit too disconnected at the moment to go fast, so that’s what we need to fix," said Verstappen.

"The all-round balance is not there at the moment, so that is costing us quite a bit of lap time. We’ll keep trying and we’ll see how good we can get it."

Red Bull, along with other teams across the grid, were unable to match Mercedes over the course of the day, as Kimi Antonelli topped both sessions, with Verstappen stating "they look quite far ahead in the short run and long run".

Team mate Isack Hadjar also struggled with balance throughout, finishing P3 in Free Practice 1 before dropping to P6 later in the day.

Red Bull Technical Director Pierre Wache believes much more work is required to stand any chance of challenging Mercedes, but that he was unsure if the team would be able to close the deficit this weekend.

"It's clear that the car balance was a lot more understeery than expected during FP1 for both drivers, even more for Max than Isack but similar problem," he said.

"Tried to fix some problem, this problem for FP2. We didn't sort out fully, it went in the right direction, but with some compromise that was not ideal. We improve quite a lot the car but still a lot of work to do.

"Looks like they [Mercedes] are very, very strong in high speed corner. I didn't check their balance of the drive, but clearly on speed profile they look very strong and it will be a big challenge to match them.

"Try to concentrate on ourselves, fix our problem first and see where we are but I'm not very confident for tomorrow."
