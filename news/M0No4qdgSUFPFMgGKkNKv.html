Почему новое соглашение Bearman с Haas так долго не перешло черту

F1.com's Lawrence Barretto explains the delay in announcing Ollie Bearman's latest Haas contract, which marks the completion of the 2027 grid.

Ollie Bearman's Formula 1 future is officially secure after Haas announced he'll be in the car for at least 2027. But what took so long?

As worst kept secrets in the F1 paddock go, Bearman continuing with Haas for 2027 is right up there.

Haas have long made clear that they wanted to keep him after he levelled up in his second season with them in 2026, outshining team mate and one-time race winner Esteban Ocon.

Of Haas' 31 points, Bearman has scored 23 of them and it has been his improved consistency, the way he motivates the team around him and the overall quality of his feedback that has really impressed. In their eyes, he's a top-line driver.

But it wasn't Haas' decision alone as to whether or not they could keep him as Bearman is part of their engine supplier Ferrari's junior programme, and they need their agreement.

Sources say Ferrari are very happy with Bearman's progress at Haas and continue to rate him highly, having invested in him since they added the Briton to their academy in 2021.

They want to keep him within the Ferrari family not just to get the return on their investment but because they believe he's talented enough to step up to the factory team at some point in the future.

Right now, though, they have no space. They tied up Charles Leclerc on a new deal earlier this year, which runs beyond 2028, and while Lewis Hamilton is only signed up until the end of 2027, he's hinted he's keen to stay.

However, there is clearly tension inside the team right now with Leclerc and Hamilton tangling again last time out in Las Vegas. And that highlights why Ferrari want someone of Bearman's quality on their books should they require someone to step up.

It's a tricky scenario for Bearman, though. He is eternally grateful to Ferrari for giving him the chance, first with a place on their academy and then as a super sub in Saudi Arabia in 2024 which really put him on the map.

And it's clear his dream is to race in red and be a factory race driver. But he's also aware that time waits for no one and he can't wait around forever.

If Hamilton signs up with Ferrari beyond the end of 2027, Bearman is facing at least two more years with Haas (taking him to four overall).

At the same time, he's going up against Rafa Camara, who joined the Ferrari junior programme at the same time as him, at Haas – and that gives Ferrari a proper comparison of their juniors.

With all that in mind, it makes complete sense for Bearman's management team to want to make sure they get the right contract.

It should be noted that this was the first big negotiation for Bearman and his management in F1, given the first deals were no-brainers for them as they got them into Ferrari and then onto the grid. It's understandable that it has taken a little time.

Sources say that it was never really about the money but rather agreeing on options and exit clauses that suit all parties.

An agreement was reached ahead of this weekend's Qatar Grand Prix, which will see Bearman race for Haas next year and what is believed to be 2028 as well, depending on, as I understand it, a certain set of circumstances including the state of play at Ferrari.

- Ferrari have someone they rate on the grid driving for a team that brings the best out of Bearman and helps drive his development. They also have access to him if they need him in the future. - Bearman has security for Haas for a couple of years. He remains on the pathway for Ferrari but has options to do something different if there's no room at the inn for the foreseeable future. - Haas get a driver who is delivering massively for them for a couple of years and who will be a good benchmark for their new rookie Camara.

And if they want to change tack at the end of next year or beyond, they can all sit down and discuss it in good faith given there is a good relationship between all stakeholders.

For now, Bearman and Haas can focus on ending this season strongly and delivering a strong campaign next year.
