Арон покидает Alpine, а «Вести» дублируются в резервной роли

Fred Vesti will take on additional reserve duties with Alpine for the final three races of the season following Paul Aron's departure from the team.

Alpine have confirmed that reserve driver Paul Aron has been released from his duties by mutual agreement.

For the last three races of the season his role will be filled by Mercedes reserve Fred Vesti, who will double up across the two teams.

Aron has reportedly opted for a return to the Formula E series, having previously competed in two races in the category in the 2023-24 season, while he also tested for the new Fortescue team in Jarama.

Aron had high hopes of becoming Estonia’s first F1 driver. A former Mercedes junior, he finished third in FIA F3 in 2023 and in FIA F2 the following year.

Those performances propelled him into the F1 reserve role with Alpine after a good showing in the 2024 Abu Dhabi rookie test.

Over the past two seasons he has completed five FP1 session with Alpine, while unusually he was also loaned out to Sauber/Audi, running a further four sessions for the Hinwil outfit.

However, there was no clear progression to a race seat at either team, and with Freddie Slater now firmly in the reserve role at Audi and Gabriele Mini impressing on his two recent FP1 outings with Alpine – including a P7 in Brazil – Aron’s chances appeared to be receding.

After two seasons on the sidelines, like others who have found themselves in similar situations, he was keen to move on and find a race seat somewhere.

"Paul hasn't raced for two years,” said Alpine managing director Steve Nielsen. “He's been our reserve and done a great job in the sim, and his reserve duties. But he wanted to go back to racing, so he's chosen his path, and we're happy to let him go and wish him luck for the future.”

While Vesti is filling the Alpine reserve role for the remainder of the season, Nielsen wouldn’t be drawn on who might get the job in 2027, although Mini would be a logical choice.

"Not sure beyond that,” he said. “No firm plans. We're looking at various alternatives, but Fred's in for the last three races as a reserve, and we’re happy to have his services.”
