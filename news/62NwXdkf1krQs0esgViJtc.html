Расселл опережает Гасли и Леклера и занимает первое место во второй тренировке на мокрой трассе

Drivers and teams finally got some meaningful running for the first time on Thursday during Free Practice 2 in Las Vegas.

George Russell claimed top spot in Free Practice 2 ahead of the Las Vegas Grand Prix, the Mercedes driver beating Alpine's Pierre Gasly and Ferrari's Charles Leclerc.

Russell posted a 1m 47.272s at the very end of the one-hour session, which was wet throughout, leaving the Briton 0.071s clear of Gasly, with Leclerc just over three tenths back.

With no competitive lap times set during a substantially wet FP1, drivers and teams were keen to make up for a lack of running around the street circuit, which remained damp once the session got underway at 2000 local time.

Several drivers ventured out in the opening minutes on the wet Pirelli tyre, with Oscar Piastri immediately reporting over the radio that intermediate tyres might not be far away.

The McLaren man became the first driver to post a time on Thursday with a 2m 02.786s, as a flurry of names began to appear on the leaderboard and lap times began to tumble.

Fernando Alonso became the first driver to exceed the limits of grip, running deep at Turn 5 and almost nudging the barrier with the front of his Aston Martin before recovering unharmed, as Lando Norris also ran deep at Turn 14 before rejoining.

The McLaren driver and former World Champion was the first to put on a set of intermediate tyres just after the 15-minute mark as a dry line began to emerge, with Piastri having lowered the benchmark down to a 1m 55.469s.

Norris immediately jumped to the top of the times with a 1m 54.615s, the Briton going even quicker on a 1m 52.432s next time around as only team mate Piastri made the switch to the green-walled tyre.

Despite running deep at Turn 1 and Turn 7, Norris continued to set the pace approaching the halfway point, lowering the best time down to a 1m 50.930s as other drivers gradually moved across to the intermediate tyre.

Leclerc became the first driver to dip below the 1m 50s barrier, posting a 1m 49.166s and reporting "I think it's probably close to slick tyres" with 20 minutes remaining, as he set a 1m 48.473s with his next lap.

Isack Hadjar moved his Red Bull briefly to the top with a 1m 48.407s, before Leclerc usurped him with a 1m 47.605s as lap times continued to drop.

With just over 10 minutes remaining a short red flag was shown to retrieve debris heading into the final corner, the offending pieces of plastic having been blown onto the circuit.

Running resumed with just six minutes remaining, prompting a flurry of cars to appear at the end of the pit lane as the light turned green and all drivers continued to use the intermediate tyre.

Hadjar was on course to go fastest before being blocked by Ollie Bearman's Haas in the final corners, with Russell jumping to the top of the times on a 1m 47.272s as the chequered flag came out.

The Briton was fastest from Gasly's Alpine, who also improved at the very end, from Leclerc, Hadjar and Piastri, with Hamilton, Norris, Kimi Antonelli (Mercedes), the second Alpine of Franco Colapinto and Alonso completing the top 10.

Lance Stroll's Aston Martin was P11 from Alex Albon (Williams) and Max Verstappen, the Red Bull driver only setting a time in the final minutes and reporting of problems with his car.

Sergio Perez was P14 for Cadillac, from Bearman, the Racing Bulls of Liam Lawson and Arvid Lindblad, the second Williams of Carlos Sainz, the Audis of Nick Hulkenberg and Gabriel Bortoleto, Valtteri Bottas (Cadillac) and Rafael Camara's Haas.
