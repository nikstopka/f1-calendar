Ферстаппен выиграл Гран-при Бахрейна в Малайзии, а Рассел завершил карьеру

Max Verstappen claimed his and Red Bull’s first win of the 2026 season at the end of a dramatic, rain-delayed Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia.

Max Verstappen converted pole position into victory during Sunday’s Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia, which was impacted by heavy rain, featured an entertaining crossover from intermediate tyres to slicks, and saw overtaking from start to finish – as well as a dramatic late retirement for George Russell.

After a delayed start and then a suspended start procedure, as several drivers struggled to maintain speed on the initial formation lap, the race eventually got under way in slippery, mixed conditions – leading to split tyre strategies and plenty of early action.

Polesitter Verstappen fell behind Mercedes pair Kimi Antonelli and Russell on the run down to Turn 1, with all three drivers running intermediate tyres, but he soon settled into a rhythm and managed to pick off the Silver Arrows either side of a Safety Car that was deployed when Valtteri Bottas spun out.

That Safety Car period represented the ideal window for the intermediate starters to switch to slick tyres, with those who lined up on slick compounds getting caught out, and threats of further rain not materialising before the chequered flag.

Verstappen controlled the final few kilometres to take the chequered flag a couple of seconds up on Antonelli, with Hamilton, Leclerc and Hadjar also maintaining formation, Piastri taking sixth, Lawson seventh and Alonso eighth.

Hulkenberg dropped out of the points late on and wound up 11th, behind Norris and Lindblad, while Stroll took 12th in his Aston Martin, Colapinto placed 13th and the Haas cars of Bearman and Ocon took 14th and 15th – the latter starting the race so well but losing out as various strategies and Safety Cars played out.

Gasly was 16th in the second of the Alpine cars, from Sainz, the penalised Bortoleto and Perez as the final finisher. Russell, Albon and Bottas were the three drivers who failed to finish – the former’s being particularly tough to take.

Russell had trimmed Antonelli’s lead to 66 points after victory in Azerbaijan, but his DNF and Antonelli’s second-place finish means that is back up to 84 points heading into the final seven rounds of the season.

Key quote

“It’s a really nice one, after the year that we’ve had and the progression that we’ve made,” said Verstappen. “I’m incredibly proud of everyone, and it’s just super nice to win here. It was very hectic with a lot of different things in the start. No power, so you have to wait for the power to kick in. But then we made the right call with the intermediates, and after that, step by step, we moved our way forward. From there onwards we could do our own race until the end again, of course, with the Safety Car, but we kept it all under control.”
