ОФИЦИАЛЬНАЯ СТАРТОВАЯ РЕШЕТКА: Кто и с какой позиции стартует на Гран-при Лас-Вегаса

The full official starting order for the 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix.

Red Bull’s Max Verstappen inherits P1 on the grid for the Las Vegas Grand Prix thanks to a five-place grid penalty for fastest qualifier Kimi Antonelli – whose Mercedes team mate George Russell joins Verstappen on the front row.

Lando Norris starts third for McLaren, with the second Red Bull of Isack Hadjar alongside. Arvid Lindblad is a career-best fifth for Racing Bulls – joined on row three by the penalised Antonelli – with team mate Liam Lawson directly behind him on the grid in P7.

Next up are the Alpines of Pierre Gasly and Franco Colapinto, while Charles Leclerc completes the top 10, one place ahead of team mate Lewis Hamilton, after both Ferrari drivers failed to progress to Q3 in Qualifying around the Las Vegas Strip Circuit.

The aforementioned Antonelli isn’t the only driver demoted for taking on new power unit components. McLaren’s Oscar Piastri, Williams’ Carlos Sainz and Aston Martin’s Lance Stroll all took 10 place penalties and start 17th, 20th and 21st respectively.

Finally, Ollie Bearman was penalised a total of 35 grid spots and starts at the back for Haas after he was unable to set a classified lap time in Qualifying.
