Что сказали команды – Квалификация в Бразилии

Пилоты и команды сообщают о сухой финальной тренировке и очень дождливой квалификации на автодроме Хосе Карлоса Паче перед Гран-при Сан-Паулу в Бразилии.

Макларен

Норрис – мастер дождливой погоды, и как только пошел дождь, он сразу же вступил в расплату за поул. Он провел квалификацию в чистоте, проезжая быстрый круг за быстрым кругом и приложив последнюю попытку, прежде чем дождь усилился и не смог ухватить поул. Это была большая попытка с его стороны, и он будет надеяться, что завтра будет еще дождь – в сухую погоду Антонелли на второй позиции может оказаться для пилота McLaren более сложной задачей. Что касается Пиастри, то он не смог завершить свой последний круг во втором квартале из-за красных флажков, упустив возможность попытаться прогрессировать.

"Это была очень сложная квалификационная сессия, условия постоянно менялись. Приоритетом было сохранять спокойствие, понимать, как шины и машина нагреваются до температуры, и избегать ошибок. Я чувствовал себя уверенно на мокрой дороге начиная с первого квартала, и я рад, что мы проехали круг вместе, когда это имело значение. Мне нравится эта трасса, но мы знаем, что нам не хватило скорости Mercedes на сухой трассе, и сегодня машина не чувствовала себя такой сильной, как раньше в выходные.

«Большое спасибо команде за то, что она продолжает усердно работать и дает мне возможность бороться за поула. Сегодня мне показалось, что у нас больше скорости на мокрой дороге и на промежуточном этапе. Условия могут сделать гонку сложной, в которой будет всего понемногу, поэтому нам нужно оставаться сосредоточенными и максимально использовать свои шансы».

«Сегодняшняя квалификация была очень сложной, особенно в начале, когда трасса была невероятно скользкой. К концу стало немного легче, но вопрос с шинами всегда был трудным, потому что я был далеко впереди по трассе, и всегда был риск, что более ранний красный флаг может что-то изменить, но мы заплатили цену, пропустив третий квартал.

«Машина чувствовала себя приемлемо на мокрой дороге, и мы были более конкурентоспособны и комфортны на сухой дороге в эти выходные, поэтому я разочарован тем, что не смог провести большую часть сессии. Мы рассмотрим, что произошло, и посмотрим, что принесет завтра погода и окончательный порядок стартовой решетки. Если она сухая, мы выглядим сильными; если мокрая, мы все еще приличны, но нам придется усердно работать всю ночь и в гонке, чтобы добиться прогресса на поле».

«Эта первое местоя — настоящий источник удовлетворения. Поздравляем Лэндо, который в очень сложных условиях проехал отличный круг в решающий момент в начале третьего квартала. Вся команда хорошо справилась с очень сложной сессией из-за быстро меняющихся погодных условий. Обидно за Оскара, которого подвел красный флаг как раз в тот момент, когда он собирался завершить круг, который, вероятно, позволил бы ему выйти в четвертьфинал. Трудно было определить правильный момент для переключения на Промежуточные шины, и красный флаг оказался вне нашего контроля.

"Мы приложили значительные усилия, чтобы привезти пакет обновлений сюда, в Бразилию, и, судя по тому, что мы видели на трех сессиях свободной практики, они обеспечивают ожидаемый нами прирост производительности. Мы добились прогресса, хотя это не значит, что у нас внезапно появится самая быстрая машина.

«Завтрашняя гонка будет открытой, стартовая сетка будет сильно отличаться от обычной из-за сочетания влажной квалификации и различных штрафов. Погодные условия будут играть решающую роль, и очень важно не допускать ошибок и правильно реагировать на все, что происходит на трассе. Сегодняшний результат дает нам большую мотивацию для решения этой новой задачи: мы сделаем все возможное, чтобы заработать ценные очки для чемпионата».

Мерседес

Рассел часто хорошо себя чувствует на мокрой дороге и регулярно занимал первое место на всех трех этапах квалификации. В третьем квартале, когда интерны вышли на поле, он захватил временный поул на первом круге, но затем Норрис пошел быстрее. Рассел прервал свой второй забег из-за пробки, а затем, когда дождь усилился, он понял, что его шанс упущен. Это очень расстроило британца, но из-за промаха на стартовой решетке он все равно стартует седьмым.

Что касается нового чемпиона мира, он был впереди, но не мог сравниться со своим товарищем по команде на мокрой дороге. Но благодаря пенальти Рассела Антонелли завтра стартует вторым.

«Это была сложная квалификация, но в то же время очень веселая. Это была моя первая мокрая квалификация в Формуле 1, поэтому я многому научился и пытался понять сцепление и линии по мере изменения условий. Возможно, временами я был слишком осторожен, а затем допустил две большие ошибки на своем последнем круге в третьем квартале, и это было обидно, поскольку там определенно было больше производительности.

"Тем не менее, завтрашний старт со второй позиции дает нам действительно хорошую позицию. Я чувствовал себя очень хорошо с машиной весь уик-энд, особенно на сухой дороге, и сегодня я многому научился, что поможет, если у нас будут одинаковые условия в гонке. Независимо от того, мокрая она или сухая, я думаю, что у нас есть хорошие шансы, и мы будем стремиться к победе. Мы хотим завоевать Кубок конструкторов и, надеюсь, сможем сделать это с сильным командным результатом".

«Очевидно, я разочарован тем, как завершилась сегодня квалификация. У нас определенно был темп, чтобы занять поул, но в сложной ситуации мы не смогли этого добиться. Учитывая обстоятельства прошлых выходных, завоевание поула здесь значило бы очень многое. На прошлой неделе мы пропустили поул на девять миллисекунд, когда мы не совсем ожидали оказаться на такой позиции, тогда как сегодня машина чувствовала себя сильной, и я чувствовал, что собираю круги, которые способны быть прямо впереди.

«Мы коллективно допустили ошибку в конце, и это расстраивает, но такие вещи случаются. Как гонщик, вы очень сильно чувствуете это разочарование в данный момент, но важно, чтобы мы держались вместе и поддерживали высокий моральный дух. В нашей игре сегодня все еще было много положительных моментов, и, в более широкой картине, это не сильно меняет ситуацию. Завтра мы стартуем на седьмой позиции, и у нас все еще есть прекрасная возможность двигаться вперед, набрать несколько сильных очков для команды и, надеюсь, завоевать Кубок конструкторов».

Тото Вольф, руководитель Mercedes-Benz Motorsport

«Сегодня были очень сложные условия, и вы должны снять шляпу перед гонщиками за их коллективное мастерство в такую ​​коварную погоду. Интерлагос может измениться очень быстро, и мы видели это на протяжении всей сессии.

«У Джорджа сегодня была скорость, чтобы завоевать поул, и очень обидно, что он не получил возможности завершить работу. Призыв прервать второй круг в конце третьего квартала был реальным решением 50 на 50. В тот момент он очень немного опережал Норриса, но догонял Албона, и мы думали, что вот-вот потеряем время. Без хрустального шара было очень трудно понять, следует ли отступить и создать пространство или продолжать идти. На следующем круге мы должны были продолжать идти, но после соревнований очень легко сказать это. Что было больше всего приятно, так это то, что Джордж был очень силен на протяжении всего этапа и заслужил поул, даже если он не получил его сегодня.

"Тем временем Кими тоже проделал хорошую работу в сложных условиях и завтра выйдет на вторую позицию. Учитывая пенальти Джорджа, мы знаем, что ему еще предстоит кое-что поработать на седьмой позиции, но темп на машине сегодня был ясным. Завтра нам нужно действовать правильно и максимально использовать возможности, которые у нас есть. Поскольку Ferrari стартует дальше на поле, мы знаем, что у нас есть возможность завоевать Кубок конструкторов здесь, но мы должны сосредоточиться на достижении результатов и принятии правильных решений".

Андрей Шовлин, путевой технический директор

"We made several small changes to the cars overnight and FP3 gave us a useful opportunity to assess those on the hottest track of the weekend so far. We finished P2 and P4 without either driver feeling they had put together a particularly good lap, so we were optimistic that, had qualifying remained dry, that we would have been fighting at the front.

"The rain arrived at the end of FP3 and continued through to qualifying. Conditions were very poor initially and it was right to red flag the session when the heavier rain arrived. Credit to the FIA too for timing the restart well and allowing us to complete the session in sensible conditions before the light deteriorated further.

"We had decent pace on both the extreme Wet and Intermediate tyres, although the balance wasn’t particularly easy for the drivers. The rear was quite weak and that made it difficult to build confidence. We had a close shave with Kimi in Q2 after leaving the switch to Intermediates a lap later than we should have, but ultimately P2 and P4 on the road is a reasonable team result in such tricky conditions.

"George was understandably frustrated not to get the opportunity to challenge Norris for pole. It was a difficult call at the end of his lap, with Albon ahead, as to whether to back off and create space or continue and risk compromising both that lap and the next. Ultimately, the rain intensified and there was no opportunity to improve.

"We’ve still put ourselves in a good position for tomorrow. We’re expecting more rain and that usually means an eventful race here. We’ve got two cars with good pace and, if we execute well, there should be plenty of opportunity to move forward and fight for the podium."

Ferrari

Both Ferrari cars took on a new ICE in Sao Paulo, meaning both Leclerc and Hamilton were set for 10-place grid drops. But then Hamilton’s car started smoking in FP3, leading to the team swapping in another new engine. That meant a back of the grid start for the British driver.

He took part in Qualifying anyway, looking to learn more about the cars in the wet and was having fun, but could not get his final lap in during Q2 due to red flags. Leclerc did make the top 10 shoot-out, winding up fourth which means a P14 start tomorrow.

"It was fun to drive in the rain today and the car felt good, especially in Q1. I always enjoy these conditions and it felt great to be out there having some close battles. It’s a shame for the penalty because I think we had a car that could have fought further up the order, but tomorrow is a new day and today the team did plenty of work getting me to the track in qualifying after what happened in FP3. Starting from the back tomorrow will make things difficult, but hopefully we’ll have similar conditions and plenty of opportunities. We’ll make sure everything is in the right place with the car and I’ll give it everything to make my way forward, do the best job I can for the team and enjoy the race."

"It was a really interesting qualifying for us, even if we are not fully satisfied with the position we finished in. Knowing that we would take a 10-place grid penalty, we were willing to be more experimental and try various things with the car in these conditions. We learned a huge amount today, and I think these learnings will be something to capitalise on tomorrow and take with us going forward.

"Starting from P14, we will do everything to fight our way forward, and with rain predicted, let’s see what’s in the cards for us in a hopefully exciting race."

"It was a very long qualifying session, but I think the Race Director handled it in the right way, as at times the conditions were truly extreme. The positive aspect on our side is that we were competitive in the wet from the very start.

"We knew we would have to take a penalty before the end of the season and, also taking the weather forecast into account, we decided to take it here with both drivers, because Interlagos is a track where we believe it is possible to move up the order. Therefore, so far, we have approached the weekend with the race very much in mind, including in terms of tyre management.

"Tomorrow will certainly be a challenge, but there will also be plenty of opportunities, particularly if the weather remains unpredictable, which looks likely. We have seen in the past that it is possible to make up a lot of places here when starting from the back. We will have to be ready to seize every opportunity."

Racing Bulls

With both Racing Bulls cars in the top 10 in practice, they looked on for another strong Saturday. But in Q2, disaster befell Lindblad, when he was told to park up with an issue. He managed to pull off behind some barriers, thus negating the need for any yellow or red flags which was clever thinking. Lawson though made the top 10 for the team, and grabbed a brilliant P5. That becomes third on the grid when penalties are applied, and gives the Kiwi racer a huge chance of a decent result at a time when he is fighting for his F1 seat.

"It's been a really good day for us. I had a very strong car throughout Qualifying, which is a big positive for the team. I had to back off on my final push lap because I was too close to Alonso, which was a shame as I think there was potentially more time to find. I’m really happy to be starting P3 in a strong position to make the most of the opportunities in tomorrow’s race. I wouldn’t mind seeing some rain tomorrow, but we know we have a quick car in both conditions, so I’m looking forward to it either way."

"It's a shame to end Qualifying this way. Unfortunately, I had to stop the car due to an issue with the Power Unit, which we'll investigate with the team later on tonight. Since the beginning of Q1, I've been feeling really good and thoroughly enjoying being in the car. I believe there would've been more to come had I been able to continue building through Q2 and Q3. Although I'd already had some experience running in wet weather in Malaysia and Singapore, I think this was my first time really pushing the car to its limits in these conditions. It's important to focus on the fact we're competitive, the car is fast, and Liam and the team have done a great job today. Let's see what tomorrow brings, but I'm confident that if we have a clean race, we can make up some ground and fight for points."

"Overall, it's been an amazing wet Qualifying session for the team today, but of course we have mixed emotions. Following Friday's Practice Sessions, we thought we were competitive, but felt there was a little more to come from the car. We now understand our Austin GP aerodynamic upgrade a little better and managed to extract another small step in performance from it, committing to the change for today. Car balance still needed improving in Sector 2, and we managed to address this and make improvements with setup changes over night. In FP3, we showed this had all come together, and we were pleased with our competitiveness in the dry.

"Qualifying was expected to be wet, and after some heavy downpours, the sessions started with the use of the full wet tyre, moving towards the intermediate tyres as the heavy rain held off. The whole package of the car, Power Unit and drivers were exceptional. We knew that if we kept everything clean, avoided mistakes and were on the right tyre at the right time, we would be able to progress through the early stages. As it turned out, we were comfortably quick all the way through Qualifying. Unfortunately, Arvid's Q2 session was curtailed with a mechanical failure. Without this, he looked to be on course to make the Q3 cut-off and a top ten start. We will have a solution for Sunday and are still confident of a successful race. Liam showed his undoubted skill in the wet, and was somewhat frustrated not to get even more out of the car after encountering traffic on his final lap in Q3. It was an impressive session in tricky conditions. His reward is a start on the second row in P3. The race is likely to be wet, and following today's performance we feel we're in a strong position for a points finish."

Audi

It was a mixed bag for Audi on Saturday. Bortoleto looked quick in FP3, but the Brazilian was one of the first to crash in Q1 in treacherous conditions down at Turn 12. He slid over the grass and hit the barriers, damaging his rear wing. Although he managed to make it back to the pits, the damage was too severe to get him back out. Hulkenberg though used all his experience to keep the car on track, making it all the way to Q3. He grabbed sixth, which becomes a very positive P4 for the Grand Prix.

“Going into qualifying, I just wanted to give it my best shot. I've raced here quite a few times, so I know the circuit well, although missing yesterday's running was obviously not ideal. The key was to keep it clean in very tricky conditions. We had a good session this morning and I felt confident straight away during qualifying, both on the full wets and the intermediates, which allowed me to put together some good laps.

“There is still a big job ahead of us: it's not going to be easy, especially with a wet race expected tomorrow. We'll need to stay sharp, remain focused and aim to bring home the best possible result.”

“It’s been a tough qualifying for me, and obviously not the way I wanted it to go. I locked up early on at turn 12, went straight into the grass and could not stop the car from hitting the wall. Unfortunately, that resulted in rear wing damage and, eventually, and end to my session. Still, I want to thank my mechanics for having tried to repair my car in time to get back to track. Tomorrow is another day, so I want to clear my head and focus on making the most of the race.”

“First of all, my heart goes out to Gabi, who had been so quick throughout the weekend. Conditions were extremely difficult in qualifying, and unfortunately he made a small mistake, which was enough to bring his session to an end. However, knowing his strength of character, I am sure he will be fighting back tomorrow.

“On the other side of the garage, Nico delivered another fantastic performance. His team gave him a great car, and he once again demonstrated his pace in the wet, showing why he is the Regenmeister, the rain master. Qualifying sixth overall and moving up to fourth on the grid following penalties for other drivers is a well-deserved reward for him and the team. “Overall, it is our best qualifying performance to date, and we are very much looking forward to tomorrow’s race.”

Red Bull

Verstappen was always starting from the back after taking on a raft of new PU components, so he took part in Q1 and then did not come back out, choosing to save wet tyres for tomorrow in case they are needed. But the Dutchman looked strong in the wet and in the dry FP3 session, so he will be one to watch from the back.

Hadjar was complaining on the radio in final practice, calling his car “undriveable” at one point. In the rain he kept his car on track and grabbed seventh, which becomes P5 when grid penalties are applied.

“We knew coming into today that Qualifying would be a little different for us given the grid penalty. Nevertheless, FP3 and Qualifying gave us a better understanding of the car and how to get it into the best possible window for tomorrow. Q1 went well and we were competitive, but with the amount of standing water on track and knowing we would be starting from the back, there was no reason to take unnecessary risks.

"Tomorrow will be a challenge. With overtake mode rather than DRS this year, making our way through the field might not be as straightforward as in previous seasons. But I’ll do everything I can to move forward and score as many points as possible. We’ve come through difficult situations before, so let’s see what we can do.”

“We took a slightly cautious approach in Q1, but made it through comfortably and showed strong pace in Q2, where we were third fastest. I felt confident going into Q3 and believed we had a real opportunity to fight for a top-three position. We decided to go out towards the end of the session, but the rain got heavier and the track conditions became increasingly difficult. Being one of the last cars on track ultimately worked against us, as there was very little grip and much more aquaplaning than we had anticipated.

"It feels like a missed opportunity because the pace was there, but the car is still competitive and P5 gives us a solid starting position for tomorrow. Hopefully the conditions allow us to race, and we’ll do everything we can to fight for a podium finish.”

“It’s been a busy day here in São Paulo, with plenty of work going on behind the scenes. The Team made a number of changes overnight, and Max’s performance in FP3 was an encouraging indication of the progress we had made. With the power unit change and the resulting grid penalty, we knew Qualifying would require a different approach for Max, particularly in the wet conditions. The focus was on learning as much as possible about the car while avoiding unnecessary risks in those tricky conditions.

"Isack secured P5 on the grid after the various penalties were applied, which gives him a solid starting position for tomorrow, although we know he was hoping for a little more after a very strong Q2. There is still work to do, but we now have a clearer understanding of where we stand and where we need to improve.

"Tomorrow will be a challenging race, particularly for Max starting from the back, but we know how unpredictable racing in Brazil can be. We will stay focused on ourselves, make the most of every opportunity and give both Max and Isack the best possible chance to move forward.”

Haas

Camara faced an incredibly tough ask to compete in his first Qualifying in such wet conditions. He span off early on and hit the barriers, damaging his front wing but the Brazilian kept his composure, made it back to the pits and came back out. He set a good enough time to make Q2, but towards the end of the session he missed his braking point, locking up and going straight on across the grass and into the barriers. However, with so many grid penalties, he will start 11th.

Bearman also went off at one point, but he recovered to make the top 10, and inherits P6. From there, he will be hoping for a big haul of points as he keeps an eye on rival Hulkenberg ahead – Haas and Audi fighting for seventh in the standings.

“It was certainly a crazy session. I learned a lot from my first rodeo here two years ago, and I, as well as the team, did a much better job this time around on the wet tyre, when to push and when to cool. We did well, and it’s a shame about Q3 as compared to our competitors, we just struggled to put the tyres in the correct window, so I didn’t have any grip, and then it was game over. I’m happy with the job that we did, there’s a bit more out there, but it’s okay, as we’ll get them tomorrow. I’m looking forward to it, getting a bit more rhythm in the rain, and we had good pace last time out in the wet – so hopefully that comes back and we can do our very best tomorrow.”

“It was a tricky one, but I’m okay. I was just a bit unlucky in the last corner, locking up, and then trying to make the corner, but it was really not possible. In a way, I’m a bit disappointed with the mistakes, as I know for the team, that’s not what they want, but overall I think it was a positive session. I think one thing we do know, we don’t want to crash tomorrow, and we just try to have a clean race, but I think we’ll go into the data, go into procedures with the team tonight and see what we can do best for tomorrow, and prepare for all the scenarios. There’s a lot to do in the race, but we need to get as ready as we can for a good race.”

“Performance-wise, I think it was a positive day. In FP3, we struggled a bit with the hotter track temperature, and we’ve seen it before, our operating window of our car is not really wide enough. In qualifying, from the get-go, it was very challenging conditions, but operationally, the team did a really good job. To do that, I think we’ve improved quite a lot, if you think about what happened in Malaysia, then we made a good step in Singapore, and here today, I’m really proud of the team. Ollie getting into Q3 is nothing short of what everyone in this team deserves. The last time we did that was in China, and he did a fantastic job. Ollie had these conditions two years ago, and he learned a harsh lesson, but he put every learning into it today and judged everything very well, P8 was a very good result.

“Rafa, as a rookie going into his home race, going straight into qualifying was extremely challenging with lots of standing water in Q1. He aquaplaned and got caught out there, but did a very good job to get out of Q1, that was brilliant. In Q2, he was very fast, maybe too fast, but again, they’re extreme conditions to put a rookie in the car. We’ve got to support him and reset, but today he showed how fast he is. In FP3, Q1, and Q2, until he had that moment, he showed exactly why we signed him for next year. On Sunday I believe both drivers can race really well together.”

Aston Martin

Stroll was another to slide off in Q1, and thus exited at the first time of asking. Alonso made it to Q2, and was the first to switch to the inters. It proved a good move, as he made it to Q3 for the first time this season. In the end he wound up ninth, despite coming out first and thus getting the best of the track conditions. It was a strong result for the veteran, who will be another hoping for more rain tomorrow.

“We took some risks in today’s Qualifying and secured our first Q3 of the season, so obviously I’m pleased with that. I went into Q3 quite hopeful, but then it rained more and we couldn't get the temperature into the tyres we needed. I don’t think we could have achieved a higher position in the end, but we made the most of our opportunities today and it's a strong result. Let’s see what we can do tomorrow as it’s expected to rain again.”

“Tricky conditions out there today, but I enjoyed Qualifying. The car felt good and it was fun to go out there in the rain. I’m happy with my last push lap, and considering our recent performance, P19 is a slightly better result for us. There’s more rain expected for tomorrow so it could get interesting and hopefully give us a chance to move forward.”

“Our first Q3 of the season, so that is a positive to take from today. We knew with the changing weather that tyre choice was going to be crucial, and the decision to put Fernando out on the Intermediate in Q2 was a good call. More rain is expected tomorrow, and we will go into the race ready to take advantage of any opportunities that come our way.”

Williams

Sainz also slid off in Q1 at Turn 12, but kept out of the barriers. He went off in the same place in Q2, which left him without a chance to improve when the red flags then came out. Albon made it all the way to Q3 for the first time this year. He wound up 10th, but starts from ninth in a rare positive for a team that is having a difficult season.

“I’m really happy with that result, and it’s great to be back in Q3. It was a difficult session overall, but we extracted the maximum from it. I’m pleased with my laps in Q1 and Q2, which were right on the limit, and the pace felt good again in Q3. The conditions were tough for everyone out there, but honestly, I enjoyed it. We usually go well in these conditions, and we needed the rain today. It’s been a great effort from everyone in the team, so that should give us confidence going into tomorrow. We have been strong on starts, so the plan is to keep it clean and race from there. Points are the target, so let’s hope it stays wet.”

“A tricky qualifying out there but the car was feeling really good. We were strong on the extreme wets as we got the tyres working and we were setting competitive lap times. Unfortunately I switched to the intermediate compound one lap too late and the red flag didn’t allow me to put a lap time on the board with that tyre, which was very frustrating but we were just unlucky. The FIA handled today well and made the right call to pause Q1, and they did well to make the most of the gap between showers. A bit of rain tomorrow would be welcome, and if we’re able to stay out of trouble, we can have an exciting race.”

“A really great effort from the whole team. It was fantastic to be here and listen to how the team was organising themselves: getting the tyres ready, making the call to go to inters, and using all the modes.

"With Alex, some of the trickiest conditions I can remember, and he delivered when it mattered most. Particularly for me, his Q2 lap and the real stress on that inter, it was a brilliant job. Carlos was on a good lap but, unfortunately, got caught in the red flag from Câmara, but I’m sure he’ll be up there tomorrow with the rest.

"Delaying the session was the right decision, so well done to F1 and the FIA on delivering a great qualifying in difficult circumstances. It looks like tomorrow we may have more of the same rain, and I look forward to it.”

Alpine

Gasly tends to go well in the wet, so it was some surprise to see him struggle at the start of Qualifying. His team mate was also up against it, with Alpine looking like they were not able to get heat into their tyres in the difficult conditions. In the end, both drivers exited in Q1 in a disappointing display. They do at least inherit a couple of places, with Verstappen and Hamilton dropping to the back.

“We were just too slow today and it's a frustrating end to our day. When it rains, usually opportunities come your way and I've had good memories in the past here in the wet conditions, so to be out in Q1 is of course very disappointing. We just lacked pace the whole session which, to me, is very odd. We struggled for pace compared to everyone else so we need to quickly look into why and hopefully improve for tomorrow where the conditions could be very similar to today. It will be a challenge from that far back but we'll review everything as a team and see what we can do in the race to progress.”

“It's been a tricky afternoon and of course we are all disappointed not to have gone through to at least Q2 in Qualifying. We were simply not as quick in the wet as we have been in the dry. We were lacking grip and overall pace in the conditions whereas others did not seem to have as much difficulty. We have a lot to review and hopefully, if the conditions are similar tomorrow, we can extract more pace on the Intermediates compared to the Extreme Wets. Understanding today's result is one thing but we also have to be prepared for the race and give it our all. It will be a long race, there are some drivers out of position to normal as a result of grid penalties, so we'll need to grab all the opportunities which might come our way.”

“It's been a very disappointing day with both cars out in Q1. We have some work to do to fully understand why we were one of the slowest cars in the wet in that session. From a strategy point of view, we had good track position and we were one of the last cars to the cross the line, which is just where you want to be with the track improving significantly lap after lap. So, to be that far off with all those considerations, and not looking competitive at all, is particularly puzzling for us. We will keep an eye on the forecast as it remains likely that the conditions will be similar tomorrow with more rain expected. We'll aim to find some solutions and give the drivers the tools they need to fight their way back up the field.”

Cadillac

Bottas was the first out at the start of the drenched Q1 segment, and slid his way around the track in what appeared to be very hairy conditions. Both cars managed to keep their sessions clean, but neither had the pace in the wet to advance. Bottas was the pick of the duo, with Perez dropping a place as he is due to start from the back thanks to taking on a raft of new PU components.

“The track conditions were tricky, but I think starting a little earlier was definitely the right call. Overall, there were a few complaints here and there, but we made good progress and felt competitive today, which was a positive sign. I was confident going into my final lap, thinking it was going to be the one, but I think I cooled the tyres down a little too much and didn’t get enough temperature into them. That was a bit unfortunate, as I feel we could have achieved a much better result. Hopefully, we’ll get some rain and see how things develop from there.”

“Some very tricky conditions today. The first two laps were more survival than qualifying, but I managed to keep it on track, and the pace looked quite promising in the early stages. We were able to get the tyres working quite quickly, but then towards the end, we started overheating the rear tyres and dropped to the back sadly. It was nice to have wet qualifying, with every other qualifying this year being dry - it always brings opportunities. Let's see what tomorrow brings for the race, as the forecast can be very unpredictable here, so we’re hoping for some action and to make the most of any opportunities that come our way.”

“An eventful day with dramatic weather conditions as we so often see here at Interlagos. During a dry FP3, we had a smooth session after electing to take a new ICE, turbo and MGUK on Checo’s car following the issue in Mexico, and both drivers completed their run plans. Qualifying was a significant challenge operationally, and huge credit to the team in the garage and on the pitwall for responding well to the shifting conditions. Both our drivers also demonstrated their wealth of experience, putting in solid laps and keeping the car on the tarmac where others struggled with the wet conditions and got caught out. We had a shot at getting through to Q2 today, but ultimately we didn’t manage to put everything together at the very end of the session when it mattered. Thank you to the fans who persevered through the delays and ultimately saw some great on-track action. We’ll regroup tonight and prepare for another unpredictable day tomorrow, with more rain forecast ahead of the race.”

Pirelli

Dario Marrafuschi, Pirelli Motorsport Director

"Today’s qualifying session provided a clear demonstration of wet-weather racing, with the cars able to exploit the Full Wet tyre during the opening phase when track conditions remained particularly challenging. The extreme wet tyre enabled running to resume after the delay, confirming its effectiveness even in the most demanding conditions and allowing the drivers to maintain a strong level of performance.

"Equally significant was the switch to Intermediates at the start of the second session. Thanks to the development work carried out over recent years on the crossover window, the Full Wet was able to remain effective even once standing water had disappeared from the circuit, making the subsequent transition to Intermediates particularly smooth as track conditions continued to improve.

"Current forecasts indicate a 60% chance of rain for tomorrow’s race, meaning a scenario similar to today’s cannot be ruled out should the Grand Prix take place wholly or partly on a wet track. In dry conditions, however, the most meaningful indications come from Friday’s second free practice session, the only one that provided representative long-run data.

"Analysis of the long runs has led us to revise the simulations carried out before the weekend. Wear proved higher than expected, resulting in greater performance loss over the course of a stint. For this reason, a two-stop strategy emerges as the preferred option, featuring two stints on Soft and one on Medium. This combination is estimated to offer an advantage of around three seconds over strategies involving all three available compounds. Using the Hard tyre alongside two Soft stints would result in a further increase in overall race time.

"The lower temperatures expected on Sunday, combined with the possibility of a track offering less grip than was seen on Friday, could further favour the softer compounds, which provide greater grip and come up to temperature more quickly. A one-stop strategy appears significantly less competitive, with an estimated race time around ten seconds slower than the optimum two-stop approach. In this context, the undercut could prove decisive, particularly for drivers switching to a softer compound, as they would be able to exploit the immediate gain in grip to make up track position."
