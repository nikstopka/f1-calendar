Haas подтверждает, что Камара будет выступать за команду в Лас-Вегасе

Rafael Camara will return for his second Formula 1 race weekend at the Las Vegas Grand Prix, having made his debut for Haas last time out in Sao Paulo.

Haas have confirmed that Rafael Camara will again race for the team at the Las Vegas Grand Prix – following on from his Formula 1 debut last time out at his home event in Sao Paulo – while Ryo Hirakawa is also set to get behind the wheel during Free Practice 1.

Following Esteban Ocon’s departure during the Mexico City Grand Prix weekend, reserve driver Hirakawa stepped in for his maiden race at the Autodromo Hermanos Rodriguez – ending the event with a point thanks to a P10 finish.

Camara – who was announced as one of Haas’ 2027 drivers in October – then made his own debut one week on at Interlagos, with the youngster managing to bring the car home amid challenging weather conditions.

Haas have since announced that the 21-year-old will again get behind the wheel of the VF-26 alongside Ollie Bearman in Las Vegas, offering another opportunity to gain further track time ahead of his maiden full-time campaign next season.

“I’m looking forward to getting back in the car in Las Vegas,” said Camara. “Brazil was a good learning experience, and now the focus is on building on that, improving step by step and making the most of every session.

“It’s a new circuit for me, so there’s a lot to learn, but I’ll do my best to keep progressing throughout the weekend.”

Hirakawa, meanwhile, will make his fourth FP1 appearance of the year for the squad in place of Bearman, two weeks on from the Japanese driver winning the 2026 World Endurance Championship with Toyota Racing.

Haas Team Principal Ayao Komatsu commented: “We're looking forward to kicking off the final triple header of the 2026 season at the famous Las Vegas strip. This street course offers very unique challenges, and we've got to be at our very best to perform there.

“In Brazil, our 2027 recruit Rafa Camara made his debut under extremely challenging conditions and showed us and the world why we signed him for 2027. We're looking forward to building on that for his second race weekend with us.

“Our reserve driver, Ryo Hirakawa, also put in an impressive performance when he was drafted in on Saturday morning in Mexico. He will be back in the car for FP1 in place of Ollie Bearman, now as the 2026 WEC World Champion.

“In recent races, we showed that we can perform at a very high level when everything is working well, and we're looking to get back to performing at the level we showed on Sunday in Mexico.”
