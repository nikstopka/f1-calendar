Джек Уайтхолл о том, почему Антонелли удивил его при первой встрече

In the latest episode of F1's Beyond The Grid podcast, comedian Jack Whitehall has shares his experiences as a Friend of F1 – including a memorable first meeting with Kimi Antonelli.

Jack Whitehall has shared the story of how Kimi Antonelli took him by surprise when they first met, with the actor and comedian insisting that the new World Champion is “not like any sportsman I’ve ever interacted with”.

After hosting F1-75 Live at the start of the 2025 season, Whitehall has since become an official Friend of F1 and even participated in the recent LEGO race held at the Las Vegas Grand Prix.

The Briton has also collaborated with Mercedes as part of a series for the team’s partner Marriott Bonvoy, meaning that he has been able to spend time with Antonelli and George Russell – and says he found himself surprised by Antonelli’s nature outside of the car.

“Because I’ve done over the last year a lot of work with Mercedes and I’ve been around them a lot, I have grown very fond of George and Kimi, and so it’s been really nice to be around them as they’ve had this kind of upturn in form,” Whitehall explained on the latest episode of official podcast F1 Beyond The Grid.

“Particularly with Kimi, watching him have this incredible season and being a little bit adjacent to it at times has been so amazing. He’s not like any sportsman that I’ve ever interacted with.

“I’ve done lots of stuff with footballers and rugby players, and there’s always an element of, I don’t know, ego and charisma. Not that Kimi doesn’t have charisma, but he’s just so unassuming and humble, and sweet and impish, and it’s insane.

“Something must just switch the minute he gets behind the wheel in that car and he just turns into an absolute beast.”

When asked if Antonelli is always pulling pranks – given that the Italian has become known for his fondness of a practical joke – Whitehall recounted the amusing story of when he first met the 20-year-old.

“He’s so silly and mischievous,” said Whitehall. “The first time I ever met with him, we were doing a little sketch in Singapore and we were filming in a hotel room.

“Everyone was kind of worried, and again this always happens when you’re filming with a sportsman – they’re all worried about whether they’re going to be in a good mood when they come in, and how long they’re going to have with you to do the little bit because they’ve got this insane schedule that they have to keep to.

“He came in, he walks in, he’s friendly to everyone, and genuinely all he was asking about was whether he could jump on the bed, because he wanted to jump up and down on the bed!

“And I was like ‘Is he doing a bit here? You’re literally like the Kimi Antonelli persona that I have in my head, and it’s just real’. He was just so unassuming, and he was grinning and fun.”

Whitehall also went on to share his experiences with other well-known names in F1 during the conversation, including Russell, Lewis Hamilton and Toto Wolff.
