Williams сохраняет Жад Жакет в составе на сезон 2027 года в F1 ACADEMY

Williams have confirmed that Jade Jacquet will remain with the team for the 2027 F1 ACADEMY season.

Williams have announced that Jade Jacquet will continue to represent the team for a second F1 ACADEMY season, with the French driver remaining with ART Grand Prix in 2027.

The 16-year-old heads into the final round of her rookie campaign in Las Vegas riding the high of her most successful weekend yet in the all-female series, having scored her first points alongside achieving a best finish of P4 in the Austin Feature race.

Jacquet also made history earlier this year when she became the first female to win a French F4 race, having also taken multiple top 20 finishes.

Looking to build on the foundations she’s made during her first season, Jacquet will remain as part of the Williams F1 Team Driver Academy, and she expressed her delight at the opportunity to return for another campaign.

“I’m really pleased to be continuing my journey in F1 ACADEMY with the Williams F1 Team Driver Academy,” she said. “I’ve loved being part of Williams and ART Grand Prix this past year, and I can’t wait to start my second season in F1 ACADEMY, taking everything I’ve learned from my rookie season and applying those skills into 2027.

“I’ve grown a lot over this season, and I feel Austin, with my P4 finish and first points, is just the beginning for me. I’m proud to be part of the Williams F1 Team Driver Academy and pleased to be lining up on the F1 ACADEMY grid with Williams and ART again next year.”

Sven Smeets, Sporting Director at Williams, added: "I’m delighted that Jade and Williams will continue our journey together in 2027 along with ART Grand Prix. She has progressed significantly this season, growing in confidence with every race, and Austin was a real highlight. We’re pleased to keep supporting her development through the Williams F1 Team Driver Academy next year, and I’m looking forward to seeing what she can achieve.”
