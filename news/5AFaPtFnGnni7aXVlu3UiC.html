Норрис оценивает прогресс McLaren перед Гран-при Лас-Вегаса

Ahead of the Las Vegas Grand Prix this weekend, Lando Norris praised McLaren's efforts to turn its 2026 fortunes around.

Lando Norris believes that McLaren have finally reached a point "where we can almost stop playing catch up", after the team took victory at the most recent race in Brazil.

Norris came out on top of a wet-dry-wet affair in the Sao Paulo Grand Prix to deliver his and McLaren's third win of the season, following back-to-back wins during the summer in Hungary and the Netherlands.

The team's most recent victory comes after a difficult patch where it generally struggled to match the pace of Mercedes, Red Bull and Ferrari.

Speaking on Wednesday's media day ahead of the Las Vegas Grand Prix this weekend, Norris believes McLaren are now almost on par with the leading teams after a troublesome start to the 2026 campaign.

"The fact we had a run of some wins in the middle of the season, the fact we won in Brazil, we're seeing a lot of the good signs," he said.

"I would still say there are some issues that are more severe and more fundamental that we're trying to attack and improve for next season. But I think considering where we are now, I think we can still be very happy with how the season has gone.

"Of course, we always want more and the expectation for us as McLaren, as double constructors' champions over the last two seasons, it's going to be very high.

"We set our own goals very high but it was a very difficult challenge to achieve the championship last season and obviously start this season as well as we would have liked.

"I think we've caught up well, the team have done an amazing job, but we also know as happy as we are currently, that we have a lot more work to do because the fight is very tough against Red Bull, Ferrari and Mercedes.

"They're all doing a very good job but it's our first time where we can almost stop playing catch up in some way and step back, have a look at things, re-analyse things and assess what is good, what can we keep, and what are the things we really need to improve on."

McLaren sit fourth in the teams' standings, just 17 points behind Red Bull, who scored a double podium in Brazil with Max Verstappen and Isack Hadjar.

Norris stated that getting P3 by the end of the season would mean "a huge amount" to the team.

"I mean we're still fighting for everything possible. It's a lot of money for the whole team, for every individual in the team. As always there's a lot of natural pressure on me and Oscar [Piastri] to go out and perform well, to bring points back to the team and have two cars up there.

He added: "Third is a big goal for us. Obviously Max is doing a good job and Red Bull are doing a good job, Isack did a good job last weekend. Got a tough fight, but we're up for it."
